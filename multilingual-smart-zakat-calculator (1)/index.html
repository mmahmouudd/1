About Zakat

What is Zakat?

🕌

One of the 5 Pillars

Zakat is the obligatory charity paid on wealth — a divine purification that fuels social justice and spiritual growth.

⚖️

The Nisab

Zakat applies once net wealth exceeds Nisab: 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver, held for a full lunar year.

💠

2.5% Rate

The standard rate is 2.5% of eligible wealth. Agricultural & business assets may differ — consult a scholar.

Our calculator consolidates every common asset — cash, bank balances, gold, silver, stocks, crypto, business inventory, receivables and trade property — then deducts your short-term liabilities to reveal your true Zakatable net, all in your chosen currency with live market rates.
