What is Nisab?

The minimum wealth a Muslim must hold before Zakat becomes obligatory: 85g of gold or 595g of silver.

🗓️

The Hawl (lunar year)

Wealth must remain above nisab for one full lunar year (354 days) before Zakat is paid on it.

🤲

The eight categories

Zakat is distributed to the poor, the needy, its collectors, reconciled hearts, freeing captives, debtors, in Allah's cause, and travellers.
