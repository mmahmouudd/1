Knowledge First

Understanding Zakat

Zakat is not merely charity — it is a divine obligation that purifies wealth, uplifts communities, and keeps prosperity in circulation.

👤

Who Must Pay?

Every adult Muslim whose zakatable wealth remains at or above the nisab threshold for one complete lunar year (hawl).

🌙

When Is It Due?

After your wealth has been held above nisab for a full Hijri year. Many choose Ramadan for its multiplied reward.

⚖️

How Is It Calculated?

Sum all zakatable assets, deduct immediate liabilities, and if the net exceeds nisab, pay 2.5% of the total.

🤲

Who Receives It?

The eight categories named in the Qur'an (9:60) — foremost among them the poor, the needy, and those in debt.
